# Support

We are here to help! If you are experiencing issues with Padel Companion or have any questions, please reach out to us.

## Contact Options

### Email Support

For direct assistance, please email us at:
**[wiklundlabs@gmail.com](mailto:wiklundlabs@gmail.com)**

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours.

### GitHub Issues

If you are technical or have found a specific bug, you can also check our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/monswiklund/padelcompanion-support/issues) page to see if it has already been reported or to open a new issue.

## Information to Include

When contacting support, please include the following information to help us assist you faster:

- **Device Model:** (e.g., iPhone 15, Apple Watch Series 9, Pixel 8)
- **OS Version:** (e.g., iOS 17.2, watchOS 10.1, Android 14)
- **App Version:** (Found in Settings > About)
- **Description of the issue:** A clear description of what happened and steps to reproduce the issue.
- **Screenshots:** If applicable.
