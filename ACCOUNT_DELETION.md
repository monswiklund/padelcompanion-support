# Account Deletion Request

We value your privacy and give you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your Padel Companion account and all associated data, you can do so using one of the methods below.

**Warning:** This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your match history, statistics, and profile information will be permanently removed.

## Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly through the app:

1. Open **Padel Companion**.
2. Navigate to **Settings**.
3. Tap on **Profile** (or your name).
4. Scroll down and tap **Delete Account**.
5. Confirm the action.

## Method 2: Manual Request

If you cannot access the app, you may request account deletion by emailing us:

1. Send an email to **[wiklundlabs@gmail.com](mailto:wiklundlabs@gmail.com)** with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
2. Please send the email from the address associated with your account so we can verify ownership.
3. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm once your data has been deleted.

## Data Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is removed:

- User profile (Name, Email, ID)
- All match history and scores
- Statistics and performance data
- Authentication credentials
