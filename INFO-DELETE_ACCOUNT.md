Padel Companion – Account Deletion Instructions

If you want to delete your Padel Companion account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request account deletion

Send an email to:

wiklundlabs@gmail.com

Use the subject line: “Padel Companion – Account Deletion Request”

Include:
	1.	The email address you used to sign in (Google or Apple)
	2.	Your username (optional)
	3.	A confirmation that you want all associated data removed

We will delete your account and all related data within 30 days.

What data will be deleted

When your account is deleted, the following will be fully removed from our systems:
	•	User profile (username, avatar if any)
	•	Email identifier used for login
	•	Match history
	•	Statistics and analytics tied to your user ID
	•	Friends list
	•	Any in-app data stored in the cloud
	•	RevenueCat entitlement data linked to your user ID

What data is retained temporarily

For legal and fraud-prevention reasons, some data may be retained for up to 30 days before permanent deletion:
	•	Error logs related to your account
	•	Crash logs not directly tied to personal information
	•	Subscription transaction metadata already processed by Google Play

Data that is not deleted

We do not store any data outside the items listed above.
Your account is fully removed and cannot be restored.
