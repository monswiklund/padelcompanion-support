Padel Companion – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [insert today’s date]

Padel Companion (“the App”) is provided by Wiklund Labs (“we”, “us”).
This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

⸻

1. Data We Collect

1.1 Account and Profile Information

Collected only if you choose to sign in with Google or Apple:
	•	Email address
	•	Profile name
	•	Internal user identifiers (Firebase UID, RevenueCat User ID)

1.2 App Data
	•	Match history
	•	Statistics and performance data
	•	Game mode selections
	•	Friends you connect with inside the app

1.3 App Interactions

Collected through analytics to understand how the App is used:
	•	Screen views
	•	Button taps
	•	Feature usage events
	•	Filter selections

1.4 Crash and Diagnostic Data

Automatically collected by Firebase Crashlytics:
	•	Crash reports
	•	Device model
	•	Operating system version
	•	Technical error details

1.5 Purchase and Subscription Data

Handled by RevenueCat:
	•	Active/inactive premium status
	•	Subscription start and renewal timestamps

We do not store or process any payment card information.

⸻

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:
	•	Authenticate users (Google/Apple Sign-In)
	•	Sync your match history and statistics
	•	Provide premium access based on your subscription
	•	Improve app performance, stability, and reliability
	•	Understand how users interact with features to improve the experience

We do not use your data for:
	•	Advertising
	•	Selling data to third parties
	•	Third-party marketing
	•	Personalized ads or targeted advertising

⸻

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data with external companies.

We only use trusted service providers to operate the App:
	•	Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, data storage, analytics, crash reports
	•	RevenueCat — subscription management

These providers process data securely on our behalf.

⸻

4. Data Security and Storage
	•	All transmitted data is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
	•	Data is stored securely in Firebase and RevenueCat.
	•	Only the minimum required data is collected.
	•	Guest users store minimal information.

⸻

5. How to Delete Your Account

You may request account deletion at any time.

Email your request to:
wiklundlabs@gmail.com
Subject: “Padel Companion – Account Deletion Request”

Please include:
	1.	The email address associated with your account (Google or Apple)
	2.	Optional: Your profile name

Upon deletion, we remove:
	•	Your user account
	•	Match history
	•	Friends list
	•	Statistics
	•	All related Firestore data
	•	RevenueCat subscription identifiers

Data that may be temporarily retained (up to 30 days):
	•	Crash logs
	•	Technical diagnostics
	•	Purchase metadata already processed by Google Play

This retention is required for fraud prevention and system integrity.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
