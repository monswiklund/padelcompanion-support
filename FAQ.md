# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General

**Q: Is Padel Companion free?**
A: Padel Companion is free to download and use for basic scoring. We offer a Premium subscription that unlocks advanced statistics, match history, and cloud sync features.

**Q: Can I use the app on my Apple Watch without my iPhone?**
A: Yes! The Apple Watch app is designed to work independently during a match. Your data will sync to your iPhone when you are back in range.

## Account & Data

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: You can delete your account directly within the app under **Settings > Profile > Delete Account**. Alternatively, you can follow the instructions on our [Account Deletion](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md) page.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and secure cloud storage (Google Firebase) to keep your data safe.

## Subscriptions

**Q: How do I restore my purchase?**
A: If you have an active subscription but the app doesn't recognize it, go to **Settings** and tap **"Restore Purchases"**.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Subscriptions are managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store).

- **iOS:** Go to iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.
- **Android:** Go to Google Play Store > Profile Icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
